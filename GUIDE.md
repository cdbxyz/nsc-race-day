# Running a race day

You are Officer of the Day. This is everything you need.

**Your phone is race kit. Arrive charged.** Same as the flags and the horn — it
is no use to anybody at 8%.

---

## Before you leave the house

**Install it.** Open the app in Safari, tap Share, tap **Add to Home Screen**.

Do this even if you have used it before. Opening it from the home screen icon
rather than the browser is what lets it work with no signal, and Nefyn has no
signal worth relying on.

**Sign in once, at home, on wifi.** Open the app and enter the club PIN. This
pulls down the boat and member lists so they are on your phone before you need
them. Ask the committee for the PIN if you do not have it.

---

## The six steps

The app walks you through the day in order. Each screen tells you what comes
next, so you can mostly just do what is on the screen.

### 0. Race day setup

Date, your name, and the two rescue crew. Everything else is optional.

If today has a trophy race on the programme, the app already knows and offers
its name. Tap **Start race day**.

### 1. Sign-on

Add every boat that is going out.

Anyone who has raced before is one tap under the search box. For someone new,
tap **New combination** and pick their class, then the helm, then the crew if
there is one.

> **Pick from the list. Do not type a name that is already there.** If Hamish
> is in the list, tap Hamish. Typing him in again makes a second Hamish, and
> the two of them split his handicap between them. Adding someone genuinely
> new is a separate tap that says so.

Sign-on is also the day's tally: at the end, the app checks that everyone who
went out has come back. A boat missing here is a boat nobody will look for.

### 2. Before racing

A short safety checklist. Tick what is done.

You can go on with things unticked — the record just shows which. It is a
record, not a lock.

### 3. Start sequence

Record the wind first: which way it is blowing **from**, and roughly how
strong. Force 3 is a gentle breeze, force 5 is when you start seeing white
caps. Best guess is fine.

Then tap **Start 10-minute sequence** as you make the first sound signal.

The phone counts down and buzzes at ten, five, one and zero. **The horn is the
signal — the phone is only there to remind you.** It shows the flag that should
be up at each stage.

If you need to postpone, tap **Postpone (AP)**. If it was a bad start, tap
**General recall**. Either way the clock resets and you start again.

### 4. The race

One big button per boat. Tap it as each boat crosses: it counts the lap, then
says **Finish** on the last one.

Tap the wrong boat? **Undo** at the top right undoes the last thing you did.
Tap it again to undo the one before. Nothing is ever really deleted, so you
cannot make it worse by undoing.

Tap a boat's name to open it if you need to mark it retired, did-not-finish or
disqualified.

When every boat is home or accounted for, tap **End race**.

### 5. Results

Positions and points are worked out for you, handicaps included.

Check it over. If a time is wrong, tap **Correct** on that boat and fix it. You
can change the wind here too.

When you are happy, tap **Publish results**. That freezes them, puts them on the
club website, and updates the winner's handicap for the season.

> If **Publish results** is greyed out, the app says underneath why — usually
> the race has not been ended yet.

### 6. Stand-down

The tally check: every boat that signed on, confirmed ashore. **You cannot
close the day until every boat is accounted for.** That is the one thing here
the app will not let you skip.

Then a closing checklist, and **Close the race day**.

## The bar at the top

The little pill next to the club name is the app talking to the club database.

| It says | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Synced · 14:32** | Everything is safely at the club. Nothing to do. |
| **3 waiting** | Three things are on your phone, on their way. Normal. |
| **Offline · 12 waiting** | No signal. Also normal at Nefyn. Carry on. |
| **Retrying · 8 waiting** | Poor signal, still trying. Carry on. |
| **N stuck** | Something will not go up. Finish the day, then tell the committee. |

**None of these mean stop.** The app records everything on your phone first and
sends it when it can. You can run an entire race day in airplane mode.

The only thing that matters: **before you put the phone away for the week, open
the app somewhere with signal and wait for it to say Synced.**

### Coloured bars

If a coloured bar appears across the top, read it — each one says what is wrong
and what to do:

- **Red, "TEST MODE"** — someone left the app in a practice setting. Nothing
  you record is reaching the club. Close the app completely and reopen it.
- **Red, "OUT OF STORAGE"** — the phone is full and something may not have
  saved. Get signal so the day can sync, then free up space.
- **Orange, clock warning** — the phone's clock is wrong, so the times being
  recorded are wrong. Fix the date and time in Settings and tell the committee.
- **Orange, "signed out"** — tap the button and enter the club PIN. Nothing is
  lost.
- **Navy, "READ ONLY"** — someone else's phone is running this day. If it
  should be yours, tap **Take over on this device**, twice.

## If something goes wrong

**Phone dies, app crashes, you drop it in the sea and get another phone.**

Everything you tapped is saved the instant you tap it. Open the app again and
it offers to pick up where you left off. Nothing is lost by force-quitting, by
the battery dying, or by the phone locking itself.

**Someone else needs to take over.** They open the app, go to the race, and tap
**Take over on this device**. Your phone keeps everything it recorded and keeps
sending it — it just stops being the one recording.

**You tapped something wrong three taps ago.** Use **Undo** on the race screen,
or **History** to see everything you have tapped in order. Anything can be
undone, including an undo.

**The one thing not to do:** do not clear the app's data or delete the app to
fix a problem. That is the only copy of anything that has not synced yet. Ask
first, always.

---

## The short version

1. Arrive charged, installed, signed in.
2. Follow the screens.
3. Pick names from the list, never type one that is already there.
4. Ignore the sync pill during the day.
5. Get signal before you put the phone away, and wait for **Synced**.
6. Never clear the app's data.

Anything else, ask the committee. Nothing in this app can be broken by tapping
the wrong thing.
